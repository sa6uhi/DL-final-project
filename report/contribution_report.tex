\section*{\color{darkgreen}Individual Contribution Summary}

\textbf{{\azfont Əskərov Nadir} --- Data \& Sequence Lead.}
Responsible for the data and sequence-processing foundation of the system,
including leakage-aware chronological splitting, preprocessing, historical
transaction sequence construction, classical machine-learning baselines, and
core evaluation definitions.

\textbf{{\azfont Fətəliyev Akşin} --- Supervised Transformer Lead.}
Responsible for the supervised FT-CAT branch, including feature tokenization,
self-attention, temporal cross-attention, focal-loss formulation, supervised
training, and coordination of the final presentation deck.

\textbf{{\azfont Nəzərov Səbuhi} --- Self-Supervised Anomaly \& Serving Lead.}
Responsible for the denoising autoencoder and the principal engineering and
deployment infrastructure, including centralized utilities, FastAPI serving,
model serialization, Docker/CI integration, and reproducibility tooling.

\textbf{{\azfont Hüseynov Habil} --- Learned Gating, Conformal \& UI Lead.}
Responsible for learned hybrid fusion, uncertainty-aware conformal triage,
DAE-focused SHAP explainability, the Streamlit analyst interface, latent-space
visualization, gating and conformal experiments, robustness evaluation, final
IEEE report compilation, and contribution-report assembly.
